TraceParticles.jl

With TraceParticles.jl you can trace charged particles in electromagnetic fields (often called test particles). The particles move independently of each other by solving the Lorentz force and they do not feed back to the electromagnetic field. This package builds on DifferentialEquations.jl, which provides solvers, dense output, callback and parallelism of ensembles of particles.

What TraceParticles.jl package provides

ToolDescription
Equations of motionlorentzforce!, guidingcentreapproximation! and the hybrid hybridgcafo!
Parameter containersFullOrbitParams, GCAParams, HybridParams — mutable ODEProblem parameters
Problem functionsprob_funcs that draw initial conditions, including importance-weighted sampling of particles from a fluid
Output functionsoutput_funcs that keep the ensemble's memory footprint small
Reduction functionsreductions that store particle batches to HDF5
CallbacksConditions and affects that terminate particles, or switch between equations of motion
PhysicsDrifts, Larmor radius, magnetic moment, pitch angle, guiding-centre ↔ full-orbit transforms
Post-processingget_observable, and HDF5 readers
High-level interfaceContainer for paremeters and functions for easy assembly and running of a trace particle experiment

Many of the tools are general and can be used to trace particles with custom equations of motion.
